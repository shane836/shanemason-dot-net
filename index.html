About

I'm Managing Partner at Mason Equity Partners, a family-owned commercial real estate development and investment firm. I oversee all aspects of investment, development, and asset management for the firm.

I leverage AI to automate everything but taste, judgement, and human communication. I find AI very useful for creating websites, slide decks, graphics, spreadsheets, data scrapers, and reporting pipelines, as long as you provide the correct context and can properly evaluate its output.

I graduated from the Haas School of Business at UC Berkeley with dual degrees in Business Administration and Interdisciplinary Studies. I currently reside in Los Angeles, California with my wife and our son.

Work

As an entrepreneur operating a boutique development firm, I've handled all aspects of the select projects that we work on. Some hats I wear include: financial analysis, negotiation, fundraising, design management, entitlements, permit expediting, construction management, and asset management.

  • Commercial Real Estate AI Tooling. I build context vaults, operating procedures, and custom software for automating tedious parts of the real estate business: research, reporting, spreadsheets, and slide decks.
  • Mason Equity Marketing and Branding. I publish my thoughts on business and development on Twitter, LinkedIn, and in a periodic newsletter. I am responsible for all firm branding materials, including pitch decks and our website masonequitypartners.com.

  • West LA Self-Storage Asset Management. I handle budgeting, FP&A, financial controls, and property management oversight for our facility we opened in 2025.
  • San Francisco Data Center Repositioning. I entitled and repositioned a 32 MW data center in San Francisco from 2013 – 2020. You can read about the property's long path to a sale below.
